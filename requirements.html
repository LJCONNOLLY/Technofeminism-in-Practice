Course Requirements & Grading

Your course grade is made up of four components:

ComponentWeight
Weekly Reading Responses & Seminar Participation30%
Signature Project Proposal (Week 11)15%
Presentation & Peer Feedback (Week 14)20%
Final Signature Project (Track A or Track B)35%

Weekly Reading Responses & Seminar Participation

Come to class prepared with 5 ideas about the readings from the week that you want to discuss.

Signature Project Proposal

Due Week 11: a short proposal identifying your chosen track, the platform you'll audit or the prototype you'll design, and your initial theoretical framing.

Presentation & Peer Feedback

Due Week 14: an in-class presentation of your project-in-progress and written peer feedback for two classmates.

Final Signature Project

Your Critical Platform Audit essay or Feminist Platform Design prototype and design document, plus a reflection document. Due Week 15. This component is graded against the rubric below.

Final Signature Project: Grading Rubric

CriterionWeightDescription
Theoretical engagement 30% Accurate, specific use of at least three course scholars/frameworks, applied precisely to your chosen case.
Argument & personal position 35% A clear, defensible argument that states your own position on the feminism-technology relationship, not a survey of the readings.
Critical AI literacy 20% Track A: honest, specific disclosure if AI was used. Track B: genuine account of what Claude Code did and didn't do well.
Reflection document 15% Your intellectual labor made visible: what you changed your mind about, and what the process taught you.

Late Policy

Reading responses are meant to prepare you for that week's discussion, so arriving without your 5 ideas ready when class starts doesn't earn credit for that week; life happens, and I'll drop your single lowest reading-response score at the end of the semester to account for it. The proposal and final project (including the reflection document) each carry a 48-hour grace period with no penalty and no questions asked; after that, work loses 3% per day late. If something bigger is going on, email me before the deadline where possible and we'll work out an extension.
