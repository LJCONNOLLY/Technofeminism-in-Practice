Learning Objectives

  • Understand the course structure and expectations
  • Define technofeminism and its core principles
  • Connect with peers and build learning community
  • Identify initial questions and interests

Overview

Welcome to Technofeminisms in Practice! This first week is about getting oriented, meeting each other, and beginning to think about what it means to approach technology through feminist and intersectional lenses.

We'll explore the syllabus interactively, discuss what drew you to this course, and start mapping the landscape of technofeminist thought together.

📚 Readings

Wajcman, Judy (2010). "Feminist Theories of Technology."

Cambridge Journal of Economics, 34(1), 143-152.

This accessible overview introduces key debates in feminist technology studies. Pay attention to how Wajcman distinguishes between different feminist approaches to technology.

Access Reading

Optional: D'Ignazio, Catherine & Klein, Lauren F. (2020).

"Introduction: Why Data Science Needs Feminism." In Data Feminism. MIT Press.

A contemporary introduction to how feminist principles can transform data science practice. Available free online.
