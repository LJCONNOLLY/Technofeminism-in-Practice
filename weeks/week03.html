Week 3

Standpoint Theory & Intersectionality in Tech

Multiple perspectives, situated knowledges, and technology

3 readings Interactive activity ~4 hours
← Week 2 All Modules Week 4 →

Learning Objectives

  • Understand feminist standpoint theory and its tech applications
  • Apply intersectional analysis to digital platforms
  • Recognize how positionality shapes technology design and use
  • Identify marginalized perspectives missing from tech development

📚 Readings

Harding, Sandra (1992). "Rethinking Standpoint Epistemology: What is 'Strong Objectivity'?"

The Centennial Review, 36(3), 437-470.

Harding articulates how marginalized standpoints can produce more objective knowledge.

Access Reading

Crenshaw, Kimberlé (1989). "Demarginalizing the Intersection of Race and Sex."

University of Chicago Legal Forum, 1989(1), Article 8.

The foundational text introducing intersectionality as an analytical framework.

Access Reading

Nakamura, Lisa (2014). "'I WILL DO EVERYTHlNG THAT AM ASKED': Scambaiting, Digital Show-Space, and the Racial Violence of Social Media."

Journal of Visual Culture, 13(3), 257-274.

Examines how race and racism operate in digital spaces.

Access Reading

Choose a platform (Twitter/X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Discord, etc.) and consider how different social positions might experience it differently based on race, gender, class, disability, etc.
