Week 4

Embodied Interaction

Phenomenology, affect, and interface design

📖 2 readings 🎥 Video 🔍 Case study
← Week 3 All Modules Week 5 →

Learning Objectives

  • Understand how bodies and technologies co-constitute each other
  • Explore affective dimensions of human-computer interaction
  • Analyze how interfaces encode assumptions about bodies
  • Consider disability justice perspectives on embodied tech

📚 Readings

Suchman, Lucy (2007). "Human-Machine Reconfigurations: Plans and Situated Actions."

Cambridge University Press. Read: Chapters 1-2

Suchman's work on situated action challenges rationalist models of HCI.

Access Reading

Hamraie, Aimi & Fritsch, Kelly (2019). "Crip Technoscience Manifesto."

Catalyst: Feminism, Theory, Technoscience, 5(1), 1-33.

A disability justice approach to technology design and knowledge production.

Access Reading
