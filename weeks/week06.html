Week 6

Automating Inequality

Algorithmic decision-making in public services

📖 3 readings 📊 Data visualization ⏱️ ~5 hours
← Week 5 All Modules Week 7 →

Learning Objectives

  • Examine algorithmic systems in welfare, housing, and criminal justice
  • Understand how automation disproportionately harms marginalized communities
  • Analyze the political economy of algorithmic governance
  • Identify resistance strategies and alternatives

📚 Readings

Eubanks, Virginia (2018). Automating Inequality.

St. Martin's Press. Read: Introduction + Chapter 1 or 2

Eubanks documents how algorithmic tools harm poor and working-class people.

Access Reading

O'Neil, Cathy (2016). "Arms Race: Going to College."

From Weapons of Math Destruction. Crown, Chapter 2.

Algorithmic systems in higher education admissions and ranking.

Access Reading

Brayne, Sarah (2017). "Big Data Surveillance: The Case of Policing."

American Sociological Review, 82(5), 977-1008.

How predictive policing algorithms entrench racial disparities.

Access Reading
