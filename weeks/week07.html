Learning Objectives

  • Understand how facial recognition technology encodes bias
  • Analyze the Gender Shades study methodology
  • Examine surveillance capitalism and racialized monitoring
  • Explore algorithmic accountability movements

📚 Readings

Buolamwini, Joy & Gebru, Timnit (2018). "Gender Shades: Intersectional Accuracy Disparities in Commercial Gender Classification."

Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, 81, 1-15.

The groundbreaking study revealing racial and gender bias in facial analysis algorithms.

Access Reading

Benjamin, Ruha (2019). "Introduction: The New Jim Code."

From Race After Technology. Polity Press, 1-32.

Benjamin introduces the concept of the "New Jim Code"—tech-mediated discrimination.

Access Reading
