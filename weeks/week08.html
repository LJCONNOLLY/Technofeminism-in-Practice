Week 8

Platform Capitalism & Gendered Labor

Invisible work, emotional labor, and digital platforms

📖 3 readings 🎧 Podcast
← Week 7 All Modules Week 9 →

Learning Objectives

  • Analyze gendered dimensions of platform labor
  • Understand emotional and affective labor in digital contexts
  • Examine global divisions of digital labor
  • Identify invisible labor sustaining platforms

📚 Readings

Duffy, Brooke Erin (2017). "The Romance of Work: Gender and Aspirational Labour in the Digital Culture Industries."

International Journal of Cultural Studies, 19(4), 441-457.

Examines how "passion" and "authenticity" mask exploitation in digital creative work.

Access Reading

Gray, Mary L. & Suri, Siddharth (2019). "Introduction: The Paradox of Automation's Last Mile."

From Ghost Work. Houghton Mifflin Harcourt.

The hidden human labor behind "automated" AI systems.

Access Reading

Roberts, Sarah T. (2019). "Understanding Commercial Content Moderation."

From Behind the Screen. Yale University Press, Chapter 2.

The traumatic, invisible labor of content moderators.

Access Reading

✏️ Assignment

Map invisible labor: Choose a platform and document all the invisible human labor that makes it function (moderation, data labeling, emotional work, etc.)
