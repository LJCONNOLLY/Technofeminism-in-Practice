Week 9

Data Colonialism & Surveillance

Extraction, privacy, and racialized monitoring

3 readings 🗺️ Interactive timeline
← Week 8 All Modules Week 10 →

Learning Objectives

  • Understand data extraction as colonial practice
  • Analyze racialized surveillance infrastructures
  • Examine privacy as differently distributed resource
  • Connect digital surveillance to longer histories of monitoring

📚 Readings

Couldry, Nick & Mejias, Ulises (2019). "Data Colonialism: Rethinking Big Data's Relation to the Contemporary Subject."

Television & New Media, 20(4), 336-349.

Introduces the concept of data colonialism.

Access Reading

Browne, Simone (2015). "Introduction" and "What Did TSA Find in Solange's Fro?"

From Dark Matters: On the Surveillance of Blackness. Duke University Press.

How surveillance technologies are racially configured.

Access Reading

Costanza-Chock, Sasha (2018). "Design Justice, A.I., and Escape from the Matrix of Domination."

Journal of Design and Science.

Connecting surveillance to the matrix of domination.

Access Reading

Assignment

Surveillance autobiography: Map surveillance technologies you encounter in a typical week. Who watches whom? For what purposes?
