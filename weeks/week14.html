Learning Objectives

  • Synthesize learning from across the semester
  • Envision technofeminist futures and practices
  • Identify next steps for continued engagement
  • Celebrate collective knowledge-building

Overview

We've traveled together through 14 weeks of technofeminist theory, practice, critique, and imagination. From Haraway's cyborgs to algorithmic audits, from data colonialism to design justice, we've built a rich landscape of knowledge.

This final week is about reflection, synthesis, and looking forward. What will you carry with you from this course? How will technofeminist principles inform your future work, research, teaching, or activism?

📚 Reading (Optional)

Ahmed, Sara (2017). "Conclusions: Keeping Up Feminist Momentum."

In Living a Feminist Life. Duke University Press, 257-274.

Ahmed offers reflections on sustaining feminist work over time. Though not specifically about technology, her insights on persistence, collectivity, and joy resonate with technofeminist practice.

✏️ Final Reflection: Where Do We Go From Here?

Write or record a brief reflection (your choice of format):

Prompts to consider:

  • What concept, reading, or activity most transformed your thinking?
  • How has your relationship to technology changed over the semester?
  • What technofeminist principles will you carry forward in your work/life?
  • What questions remain unanswered for you?
  • How might you continue engaging with these ideas?

Format Options:

  • Written: 2-3 pages
  • Audio: 5-minute voice reflection
  • Video: 3-5 minute video message to yourself (or future students)
  • Creative: Poem, collage, sketch, or other creative reflection
